## LinkedIn

I got tired of paying a monthly subscription to talk to my computer, so I built my own.

**Murmur** is a free, open-source dictation app for macOS. Hold a key, speak, and polished text is pasted into whatever app you're using: Mail, Slack, your browser, your code editor.

What it does:
🎙️ Four ways to start: hold Right Option ⌥, double-tap for hands-free, ⌃Space, or say "Hey Murmur"
🧹 Cleans up after you: removes "um" and "uh", stutters and false starts, and fixes punctuation with Apple Intelligence
✏️ Edit by voice: select text, press ⌃⌥Space, and say "make this friendlier" or "fix the grammar"
📝 Your own rules in one text file: replacements ("git hub" → GitHub), snippets, custom vocabulary
💼 Per-app tone: formal in Mail, casual in Slack
🌍 Speaks your language, works in any app, and skips password fields

And the part I care about most: **it's private by design.** Speech recognition and cleanup both run on your Mac. Your audio is never uploaded or saved. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

It needs macOS 26 on an Apple Silicon Mac, and it's MIT licensed.

⬇️ Download: https://github.com/vinoth12940/Murmur/releases/latest
⭐ Source: https://github.com/vinoth12940/Murmur

I'd love your feedback. Stars and contributions are very welcome.

## Hacker News (Show HN)

Title: **Show HN: Murmur – On-device dictation for macOS with Apple Intelligence**

URL: https://github.com/vinoth12940/Murmur

First comment:

> Hi HN. Murmur is a menu-bar dictation app I built because I didn't want to pay a subscription or send my voice to a server to talk to my Mac.
>
> You hold a key (Right Option by default), speak, and it transcribes with macOS 26's SpeechAnalyzer, cleans the text up with the on-device FoundationModels LLM (filler words, false starts, punctuation), then pastes it into the focused app with a synthesized ⌘V and restores your clipboard.
>
> Some details that might interest you:
> - Plain swiftc, no Xcode project and no dependencies
> - The model's output is length-checked and falls back to regex cleanup if Apple Intelligence is unavailable; user replacement rules always run afterwards, so they're guaranteed even if the model ignores them
> - An optional "Hey Murmur" wake word uses a separate volatile transcriber session with fuzzy matching, and only runs while idle because it shares the mic
> - It refuses to dictate into secure text fields
> - The only network request is a manual update check
>
> Architecture notes: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md in the repo. I'm happy to answer questions, and I'd appreciate bug reports.
